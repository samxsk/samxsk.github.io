Privacy policy – Seymo tools

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Seymo tools is a private application used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Seymo (@seymuello) for his own channel.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to read information about the owner's own channel and videos (titles, view counts, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) and, only when the owner explicitly approves a specific change, to upload or update his own videos.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to help the owner analyse and manage his own channel. It is stored only locally on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. The app does not access data of any other YouTube user.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
